Privacy Policy

App: Connect Dots: Puzzle Challenge (Android) · Package: com.ektatechnologies.connectdots · Developer: Ekta Technologies · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Summary

Connect Dots does not require an account, and Ekta Technologies does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game works offline; an internet connection is only needed for optional ads and purchases.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following only on your device: your current game, settings (theme, sound, vibration, timer, line width, colour letters and other game options), statistics, level and daily-challenge progress, your hint balance, and a record of completed purchase receipts so that purchased hints are not credited twice. This data is never sent to the developer. It may be included in your device's Android backup (for example Google One backup) if you have backup turned on. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases of hint packs. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details or other payment information. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on some menu screens (never during gameplay) and optional rewarded video ads that give free hints. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one. AdMob is provided by Google and automatically collects the following data to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and for analytics:

This data is encrypted in transit and is handled by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Permissions

The app only uses the Internet permission (for ads and purchases) and the advertising ID permission added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. Children

Connect Dots is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Because the developer does not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. Data held by Google can be managed at myaccount.google.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page and inside the app (Settings > About), and the "Last updated" date will change.
