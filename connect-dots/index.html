How to play

Three simple rules. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess.

1

Join the colours

Drag from a dot to draw a line to the other dot of the same colour.

2

Never cross

Lines can go up, down, left and right, but they can never cross or overlap.

3

Fill the board

Every single cell must be covered by a line. Join all colours and fill the board to win.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is the game free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads on some menu screens (never while you are solving a puzzle) and offers optional hint packs.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All puzzles work offline. Internet is only used for optional ads and purchases.

Does every puzzle have a solution?

Yes, and exactly one. Each puzzle is checked by a solver when it is created, so you can always solve it with logic.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3 free hints. Watch an optional video in the Store to get 5 more, or buy a hint pack. Hints never expire.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device and included in your Android backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.
