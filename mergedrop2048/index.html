Merge Drop 2048

A relaxing number merge puzzle for Android by Ekta Technologies.

Drop number tiles into the columns and merge equal numbers to build bigger tiles. Play 120 levels across four difficulties, a new Daily Challenge every day, or endless Quick play.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Remove Ads?
Open Settings and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google account you bought it with.

I bought a power-up pack but did not get it.
Make sure you have an internet connection and reopen the Store screen. If it still does not appear, email us with the order number from your Google Play receipt.

How do I change ad privacy choices?
Open Settings > Ad privacy choices (shown in regions where it is required).

How do I delete my data?
All game data is stored only on your phone. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to delete it.

The app closed unexpectedly.
Open it again and tap Send crash report in the message that appears, or email us. This helps us fix the problem quickly.
