Privacy Policy

App: Merge Drop 2048 (Android)  |  Developer: Ekta Technologies  |  Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Summary

Merge Drop 2048 does not require an account and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game works offline; an internet connection is only needed for optional ads and purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device: your current game, settings (theme, sound, vibration, timer and other game options), statistics, level stars, daily-challenge progress, your power-up balance (Undo, Hammer, Swap and Hint), and a record of completed purchase receipts so that purchased power-ups are not credited twice. If the app closes unexpectedly, a technical error report is also kept on the device; it is sent to the developer only if you choose to email it. This data is not sent to the developer otherwise. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your encrypted device backup.

3. Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases of power-up packs and a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details or other payment information. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

The app uses Google Play services (Google Play Billing) to process purchases, and Google AdMob to show banner ads on some screens (not during gameplay), full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded video ads that give free power-ups or a revive. AdMob may collect and use device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Rewarded video ads are shown only when you choose to watch one. Buying Remove Ads stops banner and full-screen ads.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance (PG).

6. Security

The app connects only over encrypted HTTPS connections and does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date will change.
