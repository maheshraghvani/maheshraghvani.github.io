Privacy Policy

App: Sudoku (Android)  |  Developer: Ekta Technologies  |  Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Summary

Sudoku does not require an account and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game works offline; an internet connection is only needed for optional ads and purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device: your current game, settings (theme, vibration, timer, highlight options), statistics, level and daily-challenge progress, your hint balance, and a record of completed purchase receipts so that purchased hints are not credited twice. This data is not sent to the developer. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases of hint packs. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details or other payment information. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

The app uses Google Play services (Google Play Billing) to process purchases, and Google AdMob to show banner ads on some screens (not during gameplay) and optional rewarded video ads that give free hints. AdMob may collect and use device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location to serve and measure ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK) the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Rewarded video ads are shown only when you choose to watch one.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date will change.
